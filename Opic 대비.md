## 구어체

### **1. 집 / 거주지 (Home & Living)**

You know, right now I’m actually living in a studio apartment in Yeouido, which is, like, a pretty well-known area if you think about it. To be completely honest with you, when you walk into my place, you can see three different room areas, so basically, it’s a three-room apartment. Out of all the spaces in my house, if I really had to pick just one favorite spot, it would definitely be the living room. I mean, I spend most of my free time there, just doing normal stuff like staring at the TV, reading books, or listening to some music. In my bedroom, there’s a pretty big desk, a bookshelf for my books, and a super comfy chair. Then when the weekend comes—because it always does—I just do some basic chores, like washing my clothes, vacuuming the floor, and taking out the trash.

### **2. 어렸을 때 살던 집 (Childhood Home)**

Back when I was a little kid, my whole family used to live in a two-story house out in the countryside. Geographically speaking, there was a real mountain right near our house. I mean, it was literally right there. So after school was over and we were done with classes for the day, my friends and I used to walk up that mountain all the time. Looking back, it was just what we did as kids, just hanging out near the trees simply because the mountain was right next to where we lived.

### **3. 쇼핑 (Shopping Habits)**

I realized that I’m someone who really likes buying stuff, which is why I make sure to go shopping at least once every single week. When it comes to places to shop, one of my favorite spots happens to be right in my neighborhood. Whenever I suddenly need to get something, I just head over there and check out a few different stores. The place is honestly huge, which basically means you can buy almost anything, from everyday groceries to electronic stuff. To be specific, the last time I went shopping was exactly two days ago. On that trip, I ended up buying a pair of shoes for a really decent price. I also really like online shopping too, mostly because it’s just so convenient. Though, come to think of it, I do remember ordering a T-shirt online once that had a bit of a problem.

### **4. 음악 (Music Preferences)**

When it comes to music, the one genre that I love more than anything else is definitely jazz. If I had to pick one specific artist, I’d say I’m a huge fan of Miles Davis. For me personally, putting on some jazz is one of the best ways to get rid of stress after a long day. I really like having it on in the background when I’m working out or just cleaning up around the house. Oh, and I also enjoy listening to classical music. There’s just something about it that helps my body relax and sleep so much better at night. But looking back at my life, I’ve actually liked rock music ever since I was a kid. I used to listen to music on an old MP3 player back then, but nowadays, I just use my smartphone like everyone else.

### **5. 조깅 / 공원 (Jogging & Parks)**

There’s just something really nice about putting on sneakers and going for a light jog in the park early in the morning. Naturally, the park I go to all the time is super close to my apartment. If you just walk at a normal pace from my front door, it takes about fifteen minutes to get there. The great thing about jogging is that you don’t need any fancy equipment at all. Every time I go, I see a lot of people doing their own thing, like walking their dogs or riding bikes. Inside the park, there’s this cute little coffee shop that sells really good coffee and sweet desserts. Every single time I go there, I just have a really nice time. So I try to visit that park as often as I can. Fun story—last week, I actually ran into my old high school teacher there out of nowhere! Besides the grass, the park has great stuff like a playground for kids and courts for badminton.

### **6. 커피숍 / 카페 (Coffee Shops)**

If you take a walk around my neighborhood, you’ll notice there are so many coffee shops everywhere. Out of all those options, the place I go to the most is a cafe named "Buttercup." The second you open the door and walk in, you’ll see a really simple and modern interior. Right in the middle of the room, they have two wooden tables with a bunch of green plants around them. The owner of the cafe is super friendly and always nice to customers. Plus, the overall vibe is just really relaxing, so anyone can just sit back and chill. To be honest, I go to coffee shops for a lot of different reasons, but mostly, it’s just the perfect place to hang out with friends and catch up.

### **7. 영화 (Movies & Cinema)**

I watch a lot of different kinds of movies throughout the year. But if I had to choose my absolute favorite, high-energy action movies are definitely #1 for me. That being said, I also have a soft spot for romantic comedies because they just make me laugh so easily. On the other hand, horror movies are something I try to avoid as much as possible, mostly because watching scary stuff almost guarantees I’ll have bad nightmares that night. Speaking of movies, I remember one film that came out back in 2018, and the special effects in that movie were just amazing. The reason I keep going back to my local movie theater is that it’s super clean and the seats are crazy comfortable. Whenever I go to the movies, I love going with my best friend, Anthony. Luckily, we have almost the exact same taste in movies, so after the movie ends, we usually grab some coffee and spend time talking about what we thought of it.

### **8. 여행 준비 (Trip Preparation)**

Before I actually leave for a trip, I usually spend a lot of time on the internet looking up all kinds of info. During these search sessions, I mainly focus on checking the weather, finding cool tourist spots, and looking up good local food to eat, just so I’m totally ready for the trip.

### **9. 제주도 여행 (Jeju Island Trip)**

If you were to ask me what my favorite vacation spot is, I would say Jeju Island without thinking twice. In terms of travel, getting there from Seoul takes about an hour on a plane. Out of all the things you can do on the island, my absolute favorite is waking up early to watch the sunrise at Seongsan Ilchulbong. On my last trip there, I spent some time hiking up the mountains, and I also jumped into the ocean for a swim. Besides that, I did a bunch of other fun outdoor activities all over the island. Of course, I also went to some great local restaurants to eat delicious food made by local chefs, and I even visited a famous museum. Last but not least, I met so many nice and friendly people during the whole trip.

### **10. 해외여행의 변화 (Trends in International Travel)**

Nowadays, traveling to other countries has become so much easier than it used to be in the past. Thanks to the internet, anyone can just open a browser and find tons of travel info without any effort. Plus, with smartphones, you can easily book hotel rooms and flight tickets with just a few taps on your screen. As a result, planning an overseas trip today is just infinitely more convenient than ever before.
